# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Swiss car buyers who are tired of spending hours searching listings across AutoScout24 and equivalent marketplaces. They know what they want but don't want to hunt for it. They're comfortable online, value their time, and expect premium digital experiences. They use this at home or on their phone, with intention — not casually browsing.

Secondary users: Swiss car dealerships who want to find serious, pre-qualified buyers rather than competing in a sea of listings.

## Product Purpose

AutoVerkauf reverses the car buying process. Instead of buyers searching inventory, buyers describe what they want once and verified Swiss dealerships compete by sending matching offers. The platform collects buyer requirements, distributes them to relevant dealers, organises incoming offers in one place, and facilitates communication until the buyer chooses.

The platform does not complete the sale — it creates the match.

## Brand Personality

Premium · Efficient · Swiss

The brand feels like Zurich private banking software: white space is a feature, every word earns its place, nothing is decorative. Confident without being loud. Precise without being cold. The experience should feel like the product was built by people who care deeply and removed everything that didn't need to be there.

## Anti-references

- AutoScout24 / mobile.de — cluttered, listing-heavy, visually cheap, overwhelming
- Generic SaaS (HubSpot, Intercom style) — blue CTAs, gradient cards, hero metrics, AI-cream backgrounds
- Traditional dealership sites — stock photo heavy, corporate, low-contrast, zero personality
- AutoTrader / CarGurus — marketplace-density, tab overload, banner ads aesthetic

## Design Principles

1. **Effortless over impressive.** The experience should feel easy, not like a feature showcase. Motion and transitions serve orientation, not delight theatre.
2. **One decision at a time.** Each step of the wizard asks for one type of input. Never overwhelm. Progressive disclosure over comprehensive forms.
3. **Typography does the heavy lifting.** Hierarchy through scale and weight, not decoration or color. When in doubt, make the text bigger and the surrounding space bigger.
4. **Precision is the aesthetic.** Swiss precision — alignment, spacing rhythm, consistent vocabulary — is the brand expression. Sloppiness is visible and breaks trust.
5. **Trust is structural.** Every verified dealer badge, every clean layout, every readable number tells the buyer they can trust this platform with a CHF 40,000 purchase decision.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast. All interactive elements keyboard-accessible. Respect prefers-reduced-motion — transitions become instant crossfades. Support all four Swiss national languages (DE/FR/IT/EN) — layouts must accommodate text expansion of up to 40%.
